Benfica contributed the most: 23.5 of 77.5 points, 30% of everything Portugal earned in 2021/22. Next came Sporting Braga (18) and Sporting CP Lisbon (16). Clubs by their main competition that season: 2 in the Champions League, 2 in the Europa League, 2 in the Conference League. Compared with 2020/21 (9.6), Portugal had a coefficient 3.316 higher and dropped from 5th to 7th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 4th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (20.5).
