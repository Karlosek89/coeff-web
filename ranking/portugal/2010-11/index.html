FC Porto contributed the most: 30 of 94 points, 32% of everything Portugal earned in 2010/11. Next came Sporting Braga (25) and Benfica (22). Clubs by their main competition that season: 5 in the Europa League. Compared with 2009/10 (10), Portugal had a coefficient 8.8 higher and climbed from 6th to 1st place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 2nd of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (20.5).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
