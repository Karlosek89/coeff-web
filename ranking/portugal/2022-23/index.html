Benfica contributed the most: 29 of 75 points, 39% of everything Portugal earned in 2022/23. Next came FC Porto (18) and Sporting CP Lisbon (17). Clubs by their main competition that season: 2 in the Champions League, 1 in the Europa League, 3 in the Conference League. Compared with 2021/22 (12.916), Portugal had a coefficient 0.416 lower and dropped from 7th to 8th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 5th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (20.5).
