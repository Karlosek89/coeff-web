FC Porto contributed the most: 27 of 54.5 points, 50% of everything Portugal earned in 2014/15. Next came Sporting CP Lisbon (10) and Benfica (8). Clubs by their main competition that season: 2 in the Champions League, 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2013/14 (9.916), Portugal had a coefficient 0.833 lower and dropped from 6th to 9th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 16th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (20.5).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
