Benfica contributed the most: 26 of 70.5 points, 37% of everything Portugal earned in 2012/13. Next came FC Porto (20) and Maritimo Funchal (8). Clubs by their main competition that season: 2 in the Champions League, 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2011/12 (11.833), Portugal had a coefficient 0.083 lower and dropped from 5th to 6th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 7th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (20.5).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
