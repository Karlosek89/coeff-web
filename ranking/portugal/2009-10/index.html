Portugal 2009/10 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

6th place in the season · coefficient 10.000 = 60 pts ÷ 6 clubs

Benfica contributed the most: 20 of 60 points, 33% of everything Portugal earned in 2009/10. Next came FC Porto (19) and Sporting CP Lisbon (14). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 5 in the Europa League. Compared with 2008/09 (6.785), Portugal had a coefficient 3.215 higher and climbed from 10th to 6th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 12th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (20.5).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
