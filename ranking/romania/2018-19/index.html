FCSB contributed the most: 4.5 of 9.5 points, 47% of everything Romania earned in 2018/19. Next came CFR Cluj (2.5) and Viitorul Constanta (2). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2017/18 (2.9), Romania had a coefficient 0.525 lower and dropped from 28th to 33rd place in the season ranking.
