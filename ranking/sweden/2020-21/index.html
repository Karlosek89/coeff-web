Malmö FF contributed the most: 5 of 10 points, 50% of everything Sweden earned in 2020/21. Next came Djurgårdens IF (2.5) and Hammarby IF (2). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2019/20 (5.75), Sweden had a coefficient 3.25 lower and dropped from 15th to 30th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 20th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2024/25 (11.375).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
