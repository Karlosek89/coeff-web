Chelsea contributed the most: 30 of 158.5 points, 19% of everything England earned in 2018/19. Next came Liverpool (29) and Tottenham Hotspur (26). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Champions League, 3 in the Europa League. Compared with 2017/18 (20.071), England had a coefficient 2.571 higher and stayed 1st in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 5th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2024/25 (29.464).
