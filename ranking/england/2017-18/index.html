Liverpool contributed the most: 32 of 140.5 points, 23% of everything England earned in 2017/18. Next came Manchester City (22) and Tottenham Hotspur (21). Clubs by their main competition that season: 5 in the Champions League, 2 in the Europa League. Compared with 2016/17 (14.928), England had a coefficient 5.143 higher and climbed from 2nd to 1st place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 7th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2024/25 (29.464).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
