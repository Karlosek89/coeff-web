Flora Tallinn contributed the most: 9 of 11 points, 82% of everything Estonia earned in 2021/22. Next came Levadia Tallinn (2). Clubs by their main competition that season: 3 in the Conference League. Compared with 2020/21 (1.375), Estonia had a coefficient 2.291 higher and climbed from 49th to 29th place in the season ranking. It is the country's best season since 2004/05.
