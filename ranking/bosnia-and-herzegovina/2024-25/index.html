Borac Banja Luka contributed the most: 14.125 of 18.125 points, 78% of everything Bosnia and Herzegovina earned in 2024/25. Next came Zrinjski Mostar (2) and FK Sarajevo (1.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Conference League. Compared with 2023/24 (2.25), Bosnia and Herzegovina had a coefficient 2.281 higher and climbed from 35th to 27th place in the season ranking. It is the country's best season since 2004/05.
