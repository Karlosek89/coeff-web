Dinamo Zagreb contributed the most: 9.5 of 20.5 points, 46% of everything Croatia earned in 2016/17. Next came Lokomotiva Zagreb (5.5) and Hajduk Split (4.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 3 in the Europa League. Compared with 2015/16 (4.5), Croatia had a coefficient 0.625 higher and climbed from 22nd to 18th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 8th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (7.031).
