Dinamo Zagreb contributed the most: 20 of 29.5 points, 68% of everything Croatia earned in 2020/21. Next came NK Rijeka (6) and Hajduk Split (2). Clubs by their main competition that season: 5 in the Europa League. Compared with 2019/20 (4.375), Croatia had a coefficient 1.525 higher and climbed from 22nd to 15th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 4th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (7.031).
