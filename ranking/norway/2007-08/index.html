Rosenborg BK contributed the most: 12 of 27 points, 44% of everything Norway earned in 2007/08. Next came SK Brann Bergen (9) and Vålerengens IF (4.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 5 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2006/07 (2), Norway had a coefficient 3.4 higher and climbed from 26th to 12th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 9th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2024/25 (11.812).
