Norway 2016/17 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

36th place in the season · coefficient 1.375 = 5.5 pts ÷ 4 clubs

← 2015/16All seasons2017/18 →

Odd Grenland contributed the most: 2.5 of 5.5 points, 45% of everything Norway earned in 2016/17. Next came Rosenborg BK (2) and Stabæk IF (0.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2015/16 (7.25), Norway had a coefficient 5.875 lower and dropped from 11th to 36th place in the season ranking. It is the country's weakest season since 2004/05.

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
