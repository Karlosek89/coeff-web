Alashkert FC contributed the most: 4.5 of 7.5 points, 60% of everything Armenia earned in 2021/22. Next came Ararat Yerevan (2) and FC Noah (1). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Conference League. Compared with 2020/21 (1.375), Armenia had a coefficient 0.5 higher and climbed from 46th to 39th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 6th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2024/25 (4.375).
