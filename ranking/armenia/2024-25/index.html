FC Noah contributed the most: 8.5 of 17.5 points, 49% of everything Armenia earned in 2024/25. Next came Pyunik Yerevan (4.5) and Ararat-Armenia (2.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Conference League. Compared with 2023/24 (2.25), Armenia had a coefficient 2.125 higher and climbed from 34th to 28th place in the season ranking. It is the country's best season since 2004/05.
