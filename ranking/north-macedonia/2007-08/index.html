Rabotnicki Skopje contributed the most: 4.5 of 5 points, 90% of everything North Macedonia earned in 2007/08. Next came Pobeda Prilep (0.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 2 in the UEFA Cup. Since 2004/05 it ranks 4th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (3.343).
