FK Ventspils contributed the most: 3.5 of 6.5 points, 54% of everything Latvia earned in 2013/14. Next came Skonto Riga (2) and Metalurgs Liepaja (1). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 3 in the Europa League. Compared with 2012/13 (1.25), Latvia had a coefficient 0.375 higher and climbed from 37th to 36th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 9th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2024/25 (3.875).
