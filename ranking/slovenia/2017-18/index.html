NK Maribor contributed the most: 11.5 of 18.5 points, 62% of everything Slovenia earned in 2017/18. Next came NK Domzale (5.5) and Nova Gorica (1.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 3 in the Europa League. Compared with 2016/17 (2.25), Slovenia had a coefficient 2.375 higher and climbed from 31st to 20th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 3rd of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2024/25 (9.093).
