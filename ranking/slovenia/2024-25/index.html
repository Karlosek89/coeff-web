NK Celje contributed the most: 17 of 36.375 points, 47% of everything Slovenia earned in 2024/25. Next came Olimpija Ljubljana (14.875) and NK Maribor (2.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Conference League. Compared with 2023/24 (3.875), Slovenia had a coefficient 5.218 higher and climbed from 26th to 18th place in the season ranking. It is the country's best season since 2004/05.
