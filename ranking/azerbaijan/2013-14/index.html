Qarabag FK contributed the most: 5.5 of 10 points, 55% of everything Azerbaijan earned in 2013/14. Next came Inter Baku (2.5) and Khazar Lenkoran (1.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 3 in the Europa League. Compared with 2012/13 (3), Azerbaijan had a coefficient 0.5 lower and dropped from 25th to 29th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 12th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2023/24 (5.875).
