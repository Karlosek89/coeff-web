KuPS Kuopio contributed the most: 3.5 of 5.5 points, 64% of everything Finland earned in 2020/21. Next came Ilves Tampere (1) and Inter Turku (0.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2019/20 (1.625), Finland had a coefficient 0.25 lower and dropped from 40th to 48th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 17th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (3.75).
