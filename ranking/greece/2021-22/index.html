PAOK Thessaloniki contributed the most: 17.5 of 32 points, 55% of everything Greece earned in 2021/22. Next came Olympiakos Piraeus (12.5) and Aris Thessaloniki (1). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Europa League, 3 in the Conference League. Compared with 2020/21 (5.1), Greece had a coefficient 2.9 higher and climbed from 18th to 10th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 4th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (14.2).
