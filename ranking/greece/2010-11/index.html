PAOK Thessaloniki contributed the most: 11.5 of 38 points, 30% of everything Greece earned in 2010/11. Next came Aris Thessaloniki (11) and AEK Athens (6.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2009/10 (7.9), Greece had a coefficient 0.3 lower and stayed 10th in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 6th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (14.2).
