Ukraine 2014/15 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

6th place in the season · coefficient 10.000 = 60 pts ÷ 6 clubs

← 2013/14All seasons2015/16 →

Dnipro Dnipropetrovsk contributed the most: 21 of 60 points, 35% of everything Ukraine earned in 2014/15. Next came Shakhtar Donetsk (17) and Dinamo Kiev (16). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 5 in the Europa League. Compared with 2013/14 (7.833), Ukraine had a coefficient 2.167 higher and climbed from 9th to 6th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 3rd of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2008/09 (16.625).
