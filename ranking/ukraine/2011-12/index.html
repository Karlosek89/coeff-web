Ukraine 2011/12 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

11th place in the season · coefficient 7.750 = 46.5 pts ÷ 6 clubs

Metalist Kharkiv contributed the most: 19.5 of 46.5 points, 42% of everything Ukraine earned in 2011/12. Next came Shakhtar Donetsk (8) and Dinamo Kiev (8). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 5 in the Europa League. Compared with 2010/11 (10.083), Ukraine had a coefficient 2.333 lower and dropped from 9th to 11th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 10th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2008/09 (16.625).
