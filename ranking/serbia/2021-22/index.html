Serbia 2021/22 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

9th place in the season · coefficient 9.500 = 38 pts ÷ 4 clubs

← 2020/21All seasons2022/23 →

Red Star Belgrade contributed the most: 18.5 of 38 points, 49% of everything Serbia earned in 2021/22. Next came Partizan Belgrade (15) and Cukaricki Belgrade (2.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Europa League, 3 in the Conference League. Compared with 2020/21 (5.5), Serbia had a coefficient 4 higher and climbed from 16th to 9th place in the season ranking. It is the country's best season since 2004/05.
