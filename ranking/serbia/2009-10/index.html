Partizan Belgrade contributed the most: 6.5 of 12 points, 54% of everything Serbia earned in 2009/10. Next came Red Star Belgrade (4) and FK Sevojno (1). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2008/09 (3), Serbia had the same coefficient and dropped from 19th to 23rd place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 14th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (9.5).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
