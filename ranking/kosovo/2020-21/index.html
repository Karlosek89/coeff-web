FC Drita contributed the most: 3 of 5.5 points, 55% of everything Kosovo earned in 2020/21. Next came SC Gjilani (2) and FC Prishtina (0.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 3 in the Europa League. Compared with 2019/20 (1.5), Kosovo had a coefficient 0.333 higher and climbed from 41st to 35th place in the season ranking. Since 2017/18 it ranks 7th of 9 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (3.781).
