Hapoel Beer-Sheva contributed the most: 4.5 of 9.5 points, 47% of everything Israel earned in 2019/20. Next came Maccabi Haifa (3) and Bnei Yehuda (1.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2018/19 (2.625), Israel had a coefficient 0.25 lower and dropped from 29th to 34th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 18th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2023/24 (8.75).
