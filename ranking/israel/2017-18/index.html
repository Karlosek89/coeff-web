Maccabi Tel-Aviv contributed the most: 10.5 of 22.5 points, 47% of everything Israel earned in 2017/18. Next came Hapoel Beer-Sheva (7) and Beitar Jerusalem (2.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2016/17 (6.75), Israel had a coefficient 1.125 lower and dropped from 13th to 14th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 10th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2023/24 (8.75).
