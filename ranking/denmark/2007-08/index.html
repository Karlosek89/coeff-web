FC København contributed the most: 6.5 of 20.5 points, 32% of everything Denmark earned in 2007/08. Next came AaB Aalborg (6) and OB Odense (5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2006/07 (6.125), Denmark had a coefficient 1 lower and dropped from 12th to 15th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 12th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (12.25).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
