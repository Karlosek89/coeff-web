Denmark 2011/12 — clubs and points

FC København contributed the most: 6 of 15.5 points, 39% of everything Denmark earned in 2011/12. Next came OB Odense (5.5) and FC Midtjylland (2.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 5 in the Europa League. Compared with 2010/11 (6.7), Denmark had a coefficient 3.6 lower and dropped from 11th to 22nd place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 20th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (12.25).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
