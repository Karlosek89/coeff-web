Denmark 2005/06 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

19th place in the season · coefficient 3.500 = 14 pts ÷ 4 clubs

Brøndby IF contributed the most: 7.5 of 14 points, 54% of everything Denmark earned in 2005/06. Next came FC København (3) and Esbjerg fB (2). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2004/05 (1.5), Denmark had a coefficient 2 higher and climbed from 35th to 19th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 18th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (12.25).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
