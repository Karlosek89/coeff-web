Denmark 2013/14 — clubs and points

20th place in the season · coefficient 3.800 = 19 pts ÷ 5 clubs

Esbjerg fB contributed the most: 11 of 19 points, 58% of everything Denmark earned in 2013/14. Next came FC København (7) and AaB Aalborg (0.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2012/13 (3.3), Denmark had a coefficient 0.5 higher and climbed from 22nd to 20th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 17th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (12.25).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
