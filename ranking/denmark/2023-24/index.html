FC København contributed the most: 19.5 of 34 points, 57% of everything Denmark earned in 2023/24. Next came FC Nordsjælland (10.5) and FC Midtjylland (3). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 3 in the Conference League. Compared with 2022/23 (5.9), Denmark had a coefficient 2.6 higher and climbed from 18th to 13th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 3rd of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (12.25).
