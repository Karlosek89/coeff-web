Denmark 2014/15 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

27th place in the season · coefficient 2.900 = 14.5 pts ÷ 5 clubs

AaB Aalborg contributed the most: 7.5 of 14.5 points, 52% of everything Denmark earned in 2014/15. Next came FC København (4.5) and Esbjerg fB (2.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 5 in the Europa League. Compared with 2013/14 (3.8), Denmark had a coefficient 0.9 lower and dropped from 20th to 27th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 21st of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (12.25).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
