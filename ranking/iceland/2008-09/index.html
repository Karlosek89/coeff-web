FH Hafnarfjardar contributed the most: 2.5 of 3.5 points, 71% of everything Iceland earned in 2008/09. Next came IA Akranes (1). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 2 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2007/08 (1.166), Iceland had the same coefficient and climbed from 36th to 34th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 14th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2024/25 (4.562).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
