KR Reykjavik contributed the most: 2.5 of 4 points, 63% of everything Iceland earned in 2016/17. Next came FH Hafnarfjardar (1) and Breidablik (0.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 3 in the Europa League. Compared with 2015/16 (1.125), Iceland had a coefficient 0.125 lower and dropped from 39th to 43rd place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 18th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2024/25 (4.562).
