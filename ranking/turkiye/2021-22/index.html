Galatasaray contributed the most: 18 of 33.5 points, 54% of everything Türkiye earned in 2021/22. Next came Fenerbahçe (7) and Besiktas (4). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 1 in the Europa League, 3 in the Conference League. Compared with 2020/21 (3.1), Türkiye had a coefficient 3.6 higher and climbed from 26th to 16th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 12th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2023/24 (12).
