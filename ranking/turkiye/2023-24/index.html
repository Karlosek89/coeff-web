Fenerbahçe contributed the most: 20 of 48 points, 42% of everything Türkiye earned in 2023/24. Next came Galatasaray (15.5) and Besiktas (9). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Europa League, 3 in the Conference League. Compared with 2022/23 (11.8), Türkiye had a coefficient 0.2 higher and climbed from 9th to 8th place in the season ranking. It is the country's best season since 2004/05.
