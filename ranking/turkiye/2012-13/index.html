Fenerbahçe contributed the most: 24 of 51 points, 47% of everything Türkiye earned in 2012/13. Next came Galatasaray (22) and Eskisehirspor (2). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2011/12 (5.1), Türkiye had a coefficient 5.1 higher and climbed from 18th to 7th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 5th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2023/24 (12).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
