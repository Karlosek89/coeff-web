Besiktas contributed the most: 20 of 48.5 points, 41% of everything Türkiye earned in 2016/17. Next came Osmanlispor (13.5) and Fenerbahçe (13). Clubs by their main competition that season: 5 in the Europa League. Compared with 2015/16 (6.6), Türkiye had a coefficient 3.1 higher and climbed from 12th to 7th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 7th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2023/24 (12).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
