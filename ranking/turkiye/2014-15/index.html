Türkiye 2014/15 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

14th place in the season · coefficient 6.000 = 30 pts ÷ 5 clubs

Besiktas contributed the most: 13.5 of 30 points, 45% of everything Türkiye earned in 2014/15. Next came Trabzonspor (8.5) and Galatasaray (5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2013/14 (6.7), Türkiye had a coefficient 0.7 lower and dropped from 13th to 14th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 15th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2023/24 (12).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
