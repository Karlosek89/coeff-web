Kazakhstan 2015/16 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

21st place in the season · coefficient 4.625 = 18.5 pts ÷ 4 clubs

← 2014/15All seasons2016/17 →

FK Astana contributed the most: 12 of 18.5 points, 65% of everything Kazakhstan earned in 2015/16. Next came Kairat Almaty (5.5) and Ordabasy Shymkent (0.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 3 in the Europa League. Compared with 2014/15 (3.375), Kazakhstan had a coefficient 1.25 higher and climbed from 25th to 21st place in the season ranking. It is the country's best season since 2005/06.

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
