AS Trencín contributed the most: 2.5 of 7.5 points, 33% of everything Slovakia earned in 2017/18. Next came Slovan Bratislava (2) and MFK Ruzomberok (2). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 3 in the Europa League. Compared with 2016/17 (2.125), Slovakia had a coefficient 0.25 lower and dropped from 32nd to 36th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 18th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2022/23 (6).
