FBK Kaunas contributed the most: 3.5 of 7.5 points, 47% of everything Lithuania earned in 2008/09. Next came Suduva Marijampole (3) and Vetra Vilnius (1). Clubs by their main competition that season: 3 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2007/08 (1.5), Lithuania had a coefficient 1 higher and climbed from 32nd to 23rd place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 3rd of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2018/19 (2.625).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
