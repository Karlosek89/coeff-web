Dila Gori contributed the most: 3.5 of 7.5 points, 47% of everything Georgia earned in 2013/14. Next came Dinamo Tbilisi (2.5) and Chikhura Sachkhere (1). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2012/13 (1.5), Georgia had a coefficient 0.375 higher and stayed 33rd in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 5th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2011/12 (2.875).
