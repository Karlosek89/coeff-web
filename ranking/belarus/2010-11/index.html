BATE Borisov contributed the most: 13.5 of 23.5 points, 57 % of everything Belarus earned in 2010/11. Next came Dnepr Mogilev (4.5) and Dinamo Minsk (3). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2009/10 (3.375), Belarus had a coefficient 2.5 higher and climbed from 20th to 13th place in the season ranking. It is the country's best season since 2004/05.
