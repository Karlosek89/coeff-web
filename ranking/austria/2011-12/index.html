FC Salzburg contributed the most: 11.5 of 28.5 points, 40% of everything Austria earned in 2011/12. Next came Austria Wien (10.5) and Sturm Graz (5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2010/11 (4.375), Austria had a coefficient 2.75 higher and climbed from 19th to 13th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 8th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (10.4).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
