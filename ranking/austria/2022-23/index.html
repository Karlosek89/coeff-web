FC Salzburg contributed the most: 11 of 24.5 points, 45% of everything Austria earned in 2022/23. Next came Sturm Graz (6) and Rapid Wien (3). Clubs by their main competition that season: 2 in the Europa League, 3 in the Conference League. Compared with 2021/22 (10.4), Austria had a coefficient 5.5 lower and dropped from 8th to 24th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 12th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (10.4).
