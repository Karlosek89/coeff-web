Ludogorets Razgrad contributed the most: 7.5 of 13.5 points, 56% of everything Bulgaria earned in 2021/22. Next came CSKA Sofia (4.5) and Lokomotiv Plovdiv (1.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Europa League, 3 in the Conference League. Compared with 2020/21 (4), Bulgaria had a coefficient 0.625 lower and dropped from 24th to 30th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 15th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2005/06 (8.75).
