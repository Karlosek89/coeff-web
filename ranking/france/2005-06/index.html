Olympique Lyon contributed the most: 21 of 86.5 points, 24% of everything France earned in 2005/06. Next came RC Strasbourg (14) and Lille OSC (13). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 7 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2004/05 (11.428), France had a coefficient 0.616 lower and stayed 5th in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 15th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (18.416).
