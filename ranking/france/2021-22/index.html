Olympique Lyon contributed the most: 21 of 110.5 points, 19% of everything France earned in 2021/22. Next came Olympique Marseille (20) and Paris Saint-Germain (19). Clubs by their main competition that season: 2 in the Champions League, 2 in the Europa League, 2 in the Conference League. Compared with 2020/21 (7.916), France had a coefficient 10.5 higher and climbed from 8th to 4th place in the season ranking. It is the country's best season since 2004/05.
