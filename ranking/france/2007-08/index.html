France 2007/08 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

10th place in the season · coefficient 6.928 = 48.5 pts ÷ 7 clubs

← 2006/07All seasons2008/09 →

Olympique Lyon contributed the most: 12 of 48.5 points, 25% of everything France earned in 2007/08. Next came Girondins Bordeaux (12) and Olympique Marseille (12). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 6 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2006/07 (10), France had a coefficient 3.072 lower and dropped from 5th to 10th place in the season ranking. It is the country's weakest season since 2004/05.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
