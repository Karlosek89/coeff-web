Olympique Lyon contributed the most: 15 of 80 points, 19% of everything France earned in 2006/07. Next came Lille OSC (13) and Paris Saint-Germain (13). Clubs by their main competition that season: 2 in the Champions League, 6 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2005/06 (10.812), France had a coefficient 0.812 lower and stayed 5th in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 19th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (18.416).
