Paris Saint-Germain contributed the most: 16 of 77 points, 21% of everything France earned in 2008/09. Next came AS Saint-Étienne (15) and Olympique Marseille (14). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 6 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2007/08 (6.928), France had a coefficient 4.072 higher and climbed from 10th to 6th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 13th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (18.416).
