Glasgow Rangers contributed the most: 6.5 of 17.5 points, 37% of everything Scotland earned in 2022/23. Next came Celtic (6) and Hearts FC (4). Clubs by their main competition that season: 2 in the Champions League, 3 in the Conference League. Compared with 2021/22 (7.9), Scotland had a coefficient 4.4 lower and dropped from 11th to 29th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 17th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2007/08 (10.25).
