Scotland 2010/11 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

21st place in the season · coefficient 3.600 = 18 pts ÷ 5 clubs

Glasgow Rangers contributed the most: 12 of 18 points, 67% of everything Scotland earned in 2010/11. Next came Motherwell (3.5) and Celtic (2). Clubs by their main competition that season: 5 in the Europa League. Compared with 2009/10 (2.666), Scotland had a coefficient 0.934 higher and climbed from 25th to 21st place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 16th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2007/08 (10.25).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
