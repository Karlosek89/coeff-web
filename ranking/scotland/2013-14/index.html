Celtic contributed the most: 10.5 of 13 points, 81% of everything Scotland earned in 2013/14. Next came St. Johnstone (2.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 3 in the Europa League. Compared with 2012/13 (4.3), Scotland had a coefficient 1.05 lower and dropped from 19th to 22nd place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 18th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2007/08 (10.25).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
