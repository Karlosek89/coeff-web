Club Brugge contributed the most: 23 of 48 points, 48% of everything Belgium earned in 2014/15. Next came Anderlecht (10) and KSC Lokeren (8). Clubs by their main competition that season: 5 in the Europa League. Compared with 2013/14 (6.4), Belgium had a coefficient 3.2 higher and climbed from 14th to 8th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 7th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2024/25 (15.65).
