Hamburger SV contributed the most: 22 of 101.5 points, 22% of everything Germany earned in 2008/09. Next came Werder Bremen (22) and Bayern München (20). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 7 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2007/08 (13.5), Germany had a coefficient 0.813 lower and dropped from 3rd to 4th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 18th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (21.785).
