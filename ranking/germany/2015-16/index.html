Bayern München contributed the most: 29 of 115 points, 25% of everything Germany earned in 2015/16. Next came VfL Wolfsburg (24) and Borussia Dortmund (21). Clubs by their main competition that season: 3 in the Champions League, 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2014/15 (15.857), Germany had a coefficient 0.571 higher and climbed from 3rd to 2nd place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 8th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2025/26 (21.785).
