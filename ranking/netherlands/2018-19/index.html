Ajax contributed the most: 32 of 43 points, 74% of everything Netherlands earned in 2018/19. Next came PSV Eindhoven (8) and Vitesse Arnhem (1.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 2 in the Champions League, 3 in the Europa League. Compared with 2017/18 (2.9), Netherlands had a coefficient 5.7 higher and climbed from 29th to 7th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 13th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (19.2).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
