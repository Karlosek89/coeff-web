Netherlands 2007/08 — clubs and points

16th place in the season · coefficient 5.000 = 30 pts ÷ 6 clubs

PSV Eindhoven contributed the most: 16 of 30 points, 53% of everything Netherlands earned in 2007/08. Next came AZ Alkmaar (6) and FC Groningen (2). Clubs by their main competition that season: 6 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2006/07 (8.214), Netherlands had a coefficient 3.214 lower and dropped from 7th to 16th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 20th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (19.2).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
