AZ Alkmaar contributed the most: 16 of 57.5 points, 28% of everything Netherlands earned in 2006/07. Next came PSV Eindhoven (15) and Ajax (12). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 6 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2005/06 (7.583), Netherlands had a coefficient 0.631 higher and climbed from 10th to 7th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 14th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (19.2).
