Netherlands 2014/15 — clubs and points

Closed season · values as published by UEFA

13th place in the season · coefficient 6.083 = 36.5 pts ÷ 6 clubs

Ajax contributed the most: 14 of 36.5 points, 38% of everything Netherlands earned in 2014/15. Next came Feyenoord (10.5) and PSV Eindhoven (10). Clubs by their main competition that season: 6 in the Europa League. Compared with 2013/14 (5.916), Netherlands had a coefficient 0.167 higher and climbed from 16th to 13th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 17th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (19.2).
