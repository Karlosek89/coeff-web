St. Patrick's Athletic contributed the most: 4.5 of 7.5 points, 60% of everything Ireland earned in 2008/09. Next came Drogheda United (2.5) and Cork City (0.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 2 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2007/08 (1), Ireland had a coefficient 1.5 higher and climbed from 38th to 22nd place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 7th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2024/25 (5.343).

Points = the club's contribution to the COUNTRY coefficient (win 2, draw 1, halved in qualifying, plus bonuses) — not the club coefficient used for seeding, which has different numbers. Country coefficient = total ÷ number of clubs.

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
