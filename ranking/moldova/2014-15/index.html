Zimbru Chisinau contributed the most: 4 of 7 points, 57% of everything Moldova earned in 2014/15. Next came Sheriff Tiraspol (2.5) and Veris Chisinau (0.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2013/14 (3.375), Moldova had a coefficient 1.625 lower and dropped from 21st to 36th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 10th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2021/22 (5.25).
