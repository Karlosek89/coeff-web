Sheriff Tiraspol contributed the most: 18 of 21 points, 86 % of everything Moldova earned in 2021/22. Next came Milsami Orhei (1.5) and Petrocub Hîncesti (1.5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Europa League, 3 in the Conference League. Compared with 2020/21 (1.375), Moldova had a coefficient 3.875 higher and climbed from 45th to 21st place in the season ranking. It is the country's best season since 2004/05.
