FK Krasnodar contributed the most: 10 of 26 points, 38% of everything Russia earned in 2020/21. Next came Lokomotiv Moscow (7) and Zenit St. Petersburg (5). Clubs by their main competition that season: 2 in the Champions League, 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2019/20 (4.666), Russia had a coefficient 0.333 lower and climbed from 20th to 19th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 18th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2017/18 (12.6).
