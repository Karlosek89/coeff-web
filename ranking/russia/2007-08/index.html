Zenit St. Petersburg contributed the most: 23 of 45 points, 51% of everything Russia earned in 2007/08. Next came Spartak Moscow (12) and Lokomotiv Moscow (6). Clubs by their main competition that season: 1 in the Champions League, 3 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2006/07 (6.625), Russia had a coefficient 4.625 higher and climbed from 10th to 4th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 3rd of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2017/18 (12.6).
