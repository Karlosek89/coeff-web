Zenit St. Petersburg contributed the most: 18.5 of 65.5 points, 28% of everything Russia earned in 2010/11. Next came Spartak Moscow (18) and CSKA Moscow (16). Clubs by their main competition that season: 6 in the Europa League. Compared with 2009/10 (6.166), Russia had a coefficient 4.75 higher and climbed from 13th to 7th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 4th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2017/18 (12.6).
