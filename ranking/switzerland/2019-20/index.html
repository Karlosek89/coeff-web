FC Basel contributed the most: 20 of 32 points, 63% of everything Switzerland earned in 2019/20. Next came Young Boys (7) and FC Lugano (3). Clubs by their main competition that season: 5 in the Europa League. Compared with 2018/19 (3.9), Switzerland had a coefficient 2.5 higher and climbed from 26th to 11th place in the season ranking.
