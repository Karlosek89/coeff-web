Internazionale contributed the most: 31 of 108 points, 29% of everything Italy earned in 2009/10. Next came Fiorentina (22) and AC Milan (16). Clubs by their main competition that season: 3 in the Champions League, 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2008/09 (11.375), Italy had a coefficient 4.053 higher and climbed from 5th to 4th place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 9th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2022/23 (22.357).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
