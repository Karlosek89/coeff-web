AS Roma contributed the most: 25 of 104 points, 24% of everything Italy earned in 2017/18. Next came Juventus (23) and Lazio (17). Clubs by their main competition that season: 2 in the Champions League, 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2016/17 (14.25), Italy had a coefficient 3.083 higher and climbed from 5th to 3rd place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 6th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2022/23 (22.357).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
