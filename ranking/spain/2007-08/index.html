FC Barcelona contributed the most: 25 of 111 points, 23% of everything Spain earned in 2007/08. Next came Sevilla (18) and Getafe (18). Clubs by their main competition that season: 4 in the Champions League, 4 in the UEFA Cup. Compared with 2006/07 (19), Spain had a coefficient 5.125 lower and dropped from 1st to 2nd place in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 20th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2015/16 (23.928).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
