FC Barcelona contributed the most: 33 of 127.5 points, 26% of everything Spain earned in 2010/11. Next came Real Madrid (30) and Villarreal (25). Clubs by their main competition that season: 3 in the Champions League, 4 in the Europa League. Compared with 2009/10 (17.928), Spain had a coefficient 0.286 higher and stayed 3rd in the season ranking. Since 2004/05 it ranks 14th of 22 seasons; the country's best was 2015/16 (23.928).

Older seasons were played under different rules (competition format and bonuses changed), so points across eras are not directly comparable.
