UEFA club ranking — five-year coefficient

After season 2025/26 · window 2021/22–2025/26 · seeding for 2026/27 · 415 clubs

How many points a club has collected in European competitions over the last five seasons. Draws use it to split clubs into seeded and unseeded: the higher the club, the weaker the opponents it can draw.

"min." = the club's own European results did not even reach its country's average season coefficient (UEFA rules say 20% of the five-year one — the same number), so that value becomes its floor. Clubs from strong leagues therefore never drop far, even after years away from Europe.

This ranking does not change during the season: it seeded the pots of this season's league phases. The running coefficient for next season's draws is published by UEFA after the season ends. Note it is not the club's contribution to the country coefficient (see the country pages); qualifying does not count here and fixed points are awarded for the round reached.
