Which Polish Clubs Actually Built the Coefficient? 15 Years, Club by Club

7 August 2026

Poland's UEFA coefficient didn't rise because of one great season — it's the sum of 15 years of European results from a changing cast of clubs. Here's the full breakdown, verified against kassiesa.net.

Total: 337.5 points over 15 seasons.

Legia Warszawa alone accounts for more than a third of everything Poland has earned in 15 years — and it's the only club to show up in nearly every single season. But the story of the last two years is a new supporting cast: Raków Częstochowa went from a marginal 3.5-point contributor in 2021/22 (the season the Conference League launched) to Poland's top scorer in 2025/26. Jagiellonia Białystok sat at 1–2.5 points a season for over a decade before adding 20 points in a single year (2024/25) — the biggest one-season jump by any Polish club in this dataset.
